Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 28, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Trot ("Trot," "we," "us" or "our") collects, uses and shares information when you use the Trot iPhone app, the website at trot-app.onrender.com, and related services (together, the "Services"). Trot helps you log walks with your dog, keep your dog's profile and records, find dog-friendly places, and share outings with friends and the people who care for your dog.

By using the Services, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Services.

Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your name and email address when you create an account or sign in.
  • Your dog's profile and records: your dog's name, photo, breed, bio, notes, health entries, registration details (such as microchip, licence and rabies tag numbers), training progress, and documents you upload, such as vet records.
  • Photos: only the photos you choose or take for your dog's profile or your outings. Photos are resized on your phone before upload, which removes camera details and any location stored in the photo.
  • Content you add: outing notes, places and events you add, reports about places, and a contact email if you claim a place listing.

Location

  • Trot uses your device's precise location only while you record a walk, share a live walk with friends, or use a map or "Near me" feature. Trot asks for "While Using the App" access and never for "Always."
  • A walk you start keeps recording if you lock your phone, and iOS shows its location indicator while it does.
  • The route of a recorded walk is saved with that walk. When you share a live walk, only your latest position is kept, it is shown only to mutual friends, it is hidden near your starting point, and it expires shortly after the walk ends.

Walk activity

Walk time, distance, speed and activity type, so we can show your dog's history, totals and streaks.

Information we collect automatically

  • Usage events: simple events such as "app opened" or "walk saved," with the app version and your device family (for example, "iPhone"). Durations and distances are sent as ranges. Usage events never include your location, names or anything you type. We do not store your IP address with them.
  • Diagnostics: short app error messages, with emails, tokens and long numbers masked, so we can fix problems.
  • Identifiers: your account ID, or a random ID created on your device if you use the app without signing in.

Trot does not use advertising identifiers, third-party advertising, or third-party analytics, and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

How we use your information

  • To provide and operate the Services, including recording walks, showing maps, places and directions, and keeping your dog's profile and records.
  • To create and manage your account and send you sign-in codes.
  • To let you share outings and live walks with the people you choose.
  • To send notifications you have turned on, such as walk updates and reminders.
  • To understand how the Services are used, fix problems and improve features.
  • To keep the Services secure, prevent misuse, and comply with legal obligations.

Who can see your information

  • Your dog's care team: people you add to your dog's profile can see its outings, including full walk routes, and the first names of walkers.
  • Friends: mutual friends can see live walks you choose to share.
  • Public dog page: a dog's page is public by default and shows its photo, name, breed, bio, totals and recent outings with the walker's first name and any outing photos. Walk routes stay off the public page unless you turn them on. You can make your dog's page private in the app at any time.

How we share your information

We do not rent or sell your personal information. We share it only as described below.

  • Service providers: companies that help us run the Services, under obligations to protect your information. These include Render (hosting and database), WorkOS (emailing and checking sign-in codes, which requires your email address), and Apple (push notifications and app distribution).
  • Maps and place data: to show maps, search places and plan walking routes, the app sends the map area or search text to services such as Mapbox, CARTO and OpenStreetMap services. Like any web request, these requests carry your device's IP address. We do not send them your name, email or account ID.
  • Legal requirements: when required by law or legal process, to investigate or prevent fraud or abuse, or to protect the rights, property or safety of Trot, our users or others.
  • Business transfers: if Trot is involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction.
  • With your consent: in any other case, only at your direction or with your consent.

How long we keep information

We keep your account information, dog profiles, walks and content for as long as your account is active. Usage events are deleted after 180 days. When you delete your account, we delete your information as described below, except where we must keep some of it to meet legal obligations, resolve disputes or prevent abuse.

Your choices and rights

  • Location: you can turn location access on or off at any time in your iPhone's Settings. Features that need location, such as recording a walk, will not work without it.
  • Notifications: you can turn notifications off in your iPhone's Settings.
  • Public page: you can make your dog's page private in the app.
  • Usage events on the website: if your browser sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal, the Trot website sends no usage events. The iPhone app does not currently offer this setting.
  • Access and correction: you can view and edit your account and your dog's profile in the app, or contact us for a copy of your information.
  • Account deletion: you can delete your account in the app under Settings, Delete my account. This deletes your account, your own data, and any dogs only you own along with everything on them, and removes your sign-in record from WorkOS. Walks you logged on a dog someone else owns stay on that dog and are shown as from a former member.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights, such as the right to request access to or deletion of your information. To make a request, contact us at the address below.

Children's privacy

The Services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

How we protect your information

We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards to protect your information, including encrypted connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Third-party websites and services

The Services may link to other websites and services, such as Google Maps or trainer directories. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices, so please review their privacy policies before sharing information with them.

International users

Trot is based in the United States and hosts the Services in the United States. If you use the Services from outside the United States, your information will be transferred to, stored and processed in the United States.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date at the top, and for significant changes we will let you know in the app or by email. Your continued use of the Services after an update means you accept the updated policy.

Contact us
